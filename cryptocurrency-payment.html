Here's how you'll be able to place an order:

  1. Add your items to your shopping bag

  2. Head to checkout and select the cryptocurrency payment method

  3. Confirm your order details and click Place Order — you'll be redirected to our payment provider, Triple-A

  4. On the Triple-A payment provider page, select your preferred cryptocurrency and confirm the final amount. You may need to pay blockchain fees but you don't need to create an account with Triple-A to pay with crypto

  5. Scan the QR code or copy the wallet address and amount displayed on the screen and send the cryptocurrency of your choice from your wallet

  6. Place your order within 25 minutes to confirm the current cryptocurrency exchange rate

When our payment provider, Triple-A quotes a price in any cryptocurrency they can only honour it for 25 minutes. If the 25 minutes run out, just reload the page. Triple-A will give you a new address and an updated cryptocurrency exchange rate for your payment, with another 25 minute payment window to place your order.

Once the payment is confirmed you will be redirected to the FARFETCH order confirmation page. The confirmation is usually fast, but sometimes it may take longer than traditional payment methods — while the payment is being confirmed, please don't close the browser window

If you don't send enough crypto to place your order, don't worry. You'll be notified immediately so you can send the remaining amount to complete your order.

If you send more than the necessary amount, you’ll be able to complete your order and our payment provider, Triple-A will email you to claim your refund. For more information, view the refunds in cryptocurrency section below.

Sending cryptocurrency to the wrong address or network

Your funds will be lost if you send crypto to the wrong address or use the wrong network. To minimise this risk, please make sure you scan the QR code with your wallet and that you’re using the network indicated in the payment form.

Before sending or receiving crypto, check the address, cryptocurrency, amount and network. Please always double-check the characters at the beginning and end of the address. These should be exactly the same as the address you're sending to, or receiving from.

If any of the information used for payment is incorrect, like the network or wallet address, neither FARFECTH nor Triple-A will be responsible. Crypto cannot be retrieved due to the irreversible nature of blockchain transactions.

Once your return has been accepted by our brand or partner boutique, you will receive an email from our crypto payment provider including a link to claim your funds. In this link, you will be directed to a form and prompted to enter your crypto address.

You will be refunded the price of the item you returned, in the same cryptocurrency you paid in, at the current exchange rate. Due to fluctuations in the value of the cryptocurrency, the number of tokens you receive when refunded may be different from the number of tokens you paid with. 

When a refund is successfully claimed by you, the cryptocurrency should return to your wallet within the same business day, depending on how congested the blockchain is.

Sending the incorrect amount in cryptocurrency  

If you overpaid in cryptocurrency, your order will be processed and Triple-A will automatically email you to refund the excess amount. If you didn’t send enough cryptocurrency, you have 24 hours to top up your payment. Triple-A will automatically email you to return the amount if the remaining cryptocurrency isn't sent. 

Due to a number of reasons, you may experience an invalid payment. This will be investigated and Triple-A's team may contact you for more information. If the payment was received, you can expect a refund within 5 - 7 business days.

For security reasons, orders above a specific amount will be subject to the Know Your Customer (KYC) process where you will need to provide an identification document to verify your identity.

This process is quick and you only need to upload a photo of an identity document right at the beginning of the payment.

Triple-A processes cryptocurrency payments on FARFETCH's behalf, supporting Bitcoin (BTC), Bitcoin Lightning (LN BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Tether (USDT), USD Coin (USDC) and more.
